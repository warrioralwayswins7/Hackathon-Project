Autonomous Care Coordination Workflows

How the multi-agent swarm continuously synchronizes Clinic → Lab → Pharmacy pathways

1. Monitor Agent

Continuously tracks task due dates, lab result arrival webhooks, and pharmacy dispensing logs. When a step exceeds its safe clinical window, it raises an event trigger explaining the root cause.

Status: Active • Scanning 7 Milestones

2. Care Planner Agent

Dynamically reorders follow-up queues using urgency, risk factors, and provider dependencies. If a clinic closes or lab is delayed, it calculates alternative slots and explains the rationale for rescheduling.

Status: Heuristic Dependency Engine

3. Coordinator Agent

Assigns actionable follow-up tasks to the right authorised staff member (e.g., Asha Nurse). Prepares ready-to-execute recommendations without autonomously contacting patients or modifying orders without human sign-off.

Status: Human-in-the-Loop Guardrail
